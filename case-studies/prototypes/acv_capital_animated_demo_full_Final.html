Quick answers to common questions about your floor plan.

Quick answers to common questions about floor plan management.

How is interest calculated on my floor plan?
Interest accrues daily on the outstanding principal balance at 10.5% APR. The clock starts on the floor date — the day your advance is made — and continues until the vehicle is paid off. You can see the daily interest amount on each vehicle's detail page.
What happens if a vehicle goes past due?
Once the maturity date passes, the vehicle moves to the default-rate tier (14.5% APR) and a one-time past-due fee of $25–$50 is assessed depending on days late. Continued past-due status may affect your credit line standing and could limit access to new advances.
How do I extend a vehicle's term?
Open the vehicle's detail page from the Inventory tab and click "Extend Term." Standard extensions add 30 days to the maturity date for a flat fee (typically $50–$75). Approval is automatic for accounts in good standing.
What's the difference between ACH, Wire, and F2F payments?
ACH is the standard option — free, posts in 1–2 business days. Wire arrives within 30 minutes for a 1.5% fee (capped at $25), useful for urgent payoffs. F2F (floor plan-to-floor plan) transfers your balance to another financing line — same-day, no fee.
When does interest start accruing on a new vehicle?
Interest begins accruing on the floor date, which is the day the principal advance is made — typically the day after the auction closes. The floor fee and title processing fee are also assessed on that date.
How do I request a title?
Once your payoff clears, titles are released automatically. If we already have the title on hand, you can request release immediately after payment — typically processed within 1 business day. For urgent requests, email titles@acvauctions.com.
Can I make a partial payment (curtailment) on a vehicle?
Yes. From a vehicle's detail page, choose "Request Curtailment" to pay down a portion of the outstanding balance. Curtailments reduce the principal and therefore the daily interest accrual on the remaining balance, but don't change the maturity date.
How do I increase my credit line?
Submit a request through the Contact form above with subject "Credit limit increase." Decisions typically take 3–5 business days and are based on payment history, inventory turnover, default rate, and dealer score. You can also reach your account manager directly for time-sensitive increases.
